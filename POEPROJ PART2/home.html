We are a family-owned farm located in Disaneng, North West, South Africa, founded in 1984 by two brothers and two sisters who shared a passion for agriculture and self-sufficiency. What began as a small backyard poultry operation has grown, over four decades, into a full-scale agricultural enterprise. Today, our farm specializes in producing high-quality crops and livestock, including cattle, goats, sheep, and poultry, alongside our own in-house abattoir for meat processing. Beyond farming, we also supply farm equipment such as tractors and offer construction services, including poultry houses and fencing, to support fellow farmers in our community. We take pride in our sustainable farming practices and our commitment to providing fresh, healthy food and reliable services to Disaneng, Mafikeng, and the greater North West region.
